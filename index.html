What you get

  • No account needed

    You're signed in automatically the first time you open the app. Your random name stays the same on your device.

  • One shared room

    Everyone lands in the same conversation. No servers to pick, no invites, no groups to manage.

  • Replies, emoji and GIFs

    Press and hold a message to reply to it, and use the built-in emoji and GIF picker to say it your way.

  • Light and dark mode

    Switch themes from the top bar. The app remembers your choice.

How to install

OneWay isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install the file yourself. It takes about a minute.

  1. Download the APKTap the download button above on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded fileFind it in your notifications or your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow the installAndroid will ask permission to install apps from your browser or file manager. Allow it for this one install.
  4. Open OneWayThat's it. You're in the room.

Seeing a warning? Android and Play Protect show a warning for any app installed from outside the Play Store. That's normal for a small independent app like this one.

One room means one shared space

Everyone sees everything, and everyone is anonymous. Be kind, and don't post anything you wouldn't want the whole room to read.

Anonymous isn't the same as private. Your username isn't tied to your identity, but messages are stored so the app can show them to everyone in the room — including the person who runs it. Don't share anything you wouldn't want kept.
